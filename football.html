📜 Official Rules & Guidelines

Participants must abide by the collegiate sports code of conduct.

  • Eligibility & Registration: Participation is restricted to IIIT Vadodara students. Player registration will be conducted online with a participation fee of ₹20 per player.
  • Team Ownership & Auction: There will be 8 team owners (paying ₹200 per owner). Owners do not play for the teams they own. Owners select their team captain. Team formation takes place through an Auction on Wednesday, 21 October 2026.
  • Squad Composition: Each team must have a squad of 13–15 players, including substitutes. 11 players take the field for an 11-a-side match. The minimum number of players required to start or continue a match will be decided by the organizing committee.
  • Tournament Dates, Venues & Timings:
    • Sunday, 25 October 2026: CRPF Ground (8:00 AM–12:00 PM and 2:00 PM–6:00 PM; Break from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM).
    • Sunday, 1 November 2026: IIT Ground (4:30 PM–9:00 PM).
  • Group-Stage Structure: 8 teams divided into two groups of four: Group A and Group B. Shortened schedule: each team plays 2 group-stage matches. Group-stage matches may end in a draw.
  • Points System & Ranking: Win: 3 points, Draw: 1 point, Loss: 0 points. If tied, tie-breakers in order: Goal difference, goals scored, then head-to-head result.
  • Semi-Finals & Final: Top 2 teams from each group qualify.
    • Semi-Final 1: Group A 1st place (A1) vs Group B 2nd place (B2).
    • Semi-Final 2: Group B 1st place (B1) vs Group A 2nd place (A2).
    • Winners qualify for the Final. No third-place match will be played.
  • Match Duration & Tie Resolution:
    • Group Stage & Semi-Finals: Two halves of 20 minutes each (5 min half-time break).
    • Final: Two halves of 25 minutes each (5 min half-time break).
    • Tied Knockouts: Decided by penalty shootout (standard 5 kicks per team, then sudden death).
  • Game Rules & Substitutions: Standard football rules apply. Standard offside rule applies. Rolling substitutions permitted, with a maximum of 3 players substituted per team per match. Players must comply with referee decisions.
  • Discipline & Walkovers: Referee's on-field decision is final. 2 yellow cards = red card (player leaves, team continues with one fewer player). No grace period for late arrivals; failure to appear results in a 3–0 walkover.
  • Awards: Winner and runner-up trophies will be awarded.

⚡ 11-A-Side Tournament & Playoff Format (8 Teams)

8 Teams divided into Group A & Group B. 11 on field (squad 13-15). Top 2 from each group advance to Semi-Finals.

1. Group Stage (20 Min Halves)

• Sunday, 25 Oct at CRPF Ground.
• 2 matches per team; Win=3, Draw=1, Loss=0.
• Tie-break: GD > Goals Scored > Head-to-Head.

2. Semi-Finals (20 Min Halves)

• Sunday, 1 Nov at IIT Ground.
• SF 1: Group A 1st vs Group B 2nd.
• SF 2: Group B 1st vs Group A 2nd.

3. Grand Final (25 Min Halves)

• Sunday, 1 Nov at IIT Ground.
• Tied knockout: Penalty shootout (5 kicks + sudden death).
• Winner & Runner-up Trophies awarded.
