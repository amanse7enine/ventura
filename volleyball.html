📜 Official Rules & Guidelines

Participants must abide by the collegiate sports code of conduct.

  • Online Registration & Fee: Registrations will be online. Participation fee is ₹30/- per player (Participation fee paid by unsold players will be refunded).
  • Squad Composition & Mandatory Girl Player: Each team will consist of 7 players. At least 1 girl is mandatory in every team. The minimum and maximum number of players per team will follow the team-size rule announced by the organising committee. A team that does not meet the required team composition, including the mandatory girl player, may be disqualified under tournament rules.
  • Player Auction System: Auction date and time will be announced by the organising committee. Players will be divided into three categories: Gold, Silver, and Bronze. The points system will be announced before the auction.
  • 3-Stage Auction Rounds:
    • Stage 1: Auction of all registered players.
    • Stage 2: Auction of all unsold players from the first stage (one more chance in 2nd stage; no further auction round held thereafter).
    • Stage 3: Each team may submit names of selected players for another auction (limit announced after 2nd stage).
  • Match & Court Rules: Matches will follow standard indoor volleyball rules, except for tournament-specific changes announced by the committee. Each team will consist of 7 players at a time in the court and minimum 1 girl is mandatory in the court. Teams must follow official match rules. The referee's decision will be final.
  • Tournament Structure (10 Teams): There will be 2 pools, with 5 teams in each pool. Each team will play every other team in its own pool once (4 pool matches per team). Pool A and Pool B standings will be calculated separately.
  • Match Format & Set Scores:
    • Pool Matches: Best of 3 sets (15 - 15 - 21 points).
    • Semi-Finals: Best of 3 sets (21 - 21 - 25 points).
    • Grand Final: Best of 5 sets (21 - 21 - 21 - 21 - 25 points). Match duration, court, and substitutions will follow committee guidelines.
  • Semi-Finals & Final Qualification:
    • The top 2 teams from each pool qualify for the semi-finals.
    • Semi-Final 1: Pool A 1st Place vs Pool B 2nd Place.
    • Semi-Final 2: Pool B 1st Place vs Pool A 2nd Place.
    • Grand Final: Winners of both semi-finals advance to the Final. (The two semi-final losers may play a 3rd-place match if scheduled).
  • Pool Ranking & Tie-Breaks: Pool standings determined by match wins. If teams are tied, tie-break criteria announced before the tournament (such as set ratio and point ratio) will apply. Updates will be communicated through official WhatsApp groups.

⚡ Tournament Structure & Playoff System (10 Teams)

10 Teams divided into 2 Pools of 5 teams. 7 on court (min 1 girl mandatory). Top 2 from each pool advance to Semi-Finals.

1. Pool Stage (Best of 3 Sets)

Pool A (5 Teams) & Pool B (5 Teams).
• Sets: 15 - 15 - 21 points.
• 4 pool matches each; top 2 qualify.

2. Semi-Finals (Best of 3 Sets)

• SF 1: Pool A 1st vs Pool B 2nd
• SF 2: Pool B 1st vs Pool A 2nd
• Sets: 21 - 21 - 25 points.

3. Grand Final (Best of 5 Sets)

Winner SF 1 vs Winner SF 2.
• Sets: 21 - 21 - 21 - 21 - 25.
• Champion: Crowned Ventura 2k26 Victor!
