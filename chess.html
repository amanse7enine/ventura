📜 Official Rules & Guidelines

Participants must abide by the collegiate sports code of conduct.

  • Tournament Dates & Timings: 19th October to 6th November. Matches conducted during lunch hours and in hostels in evenings.
  • Venue: Online & Offline (Courtyard) Hybrid.
  • Online Registration: Register here for Ventura Chess and get ready to make your move: Official Chess Registration Form.
  • Tournament Format: Single-elimination knockout (winner advances, loser is eliminated). Random draw bracket with power of 2 sizing.
  • Championship Grand Final: The Final will strictly be played offline on a physical board in front of the organizing committee.
  • Game Rules & Time Control: Each match is a single game of chess. Time control is 10 minutes per player with no increment (10+0). Colors decided by committee or coin toss. Official FIDE rules apply.
  • Touch-Move Rule: Strict touch-move applies in all offline matches: a piece touched must be moved, and a piece released on a square must stay there.
  • Draw & Armageddon Tiebreak: Draws by agreement are allowed only after move 20. A drawn game is settled immediately by an Armageddon game: colors swapped, White gets 5 minutes and Black gets 4 minutes (no increment). If Armageddon is drawn, Black wins the match.
  • Offline Match Conditions: Boards, pieces, and digital clocks provided by committee. Players must report to table at scheduled time. More than 5 minutes late = forfeit. Mobile phones must be switched off and kept away.
  • Online Match Conditions: Played on Chess.com. Designated player creates unrated 10+0 challenge. More than 5 minutes late = forfeit. Winner sends game link/screenshot immediately. Chess engines or outside assistance strictly prohibited.
  • Arbiter & Authority: The arbiter's decision is final. Misconduct, abusive behavior, or arguing with the arbiter can lead to game loss or disqualification.

♟️ Single-Elimination Knockout & Rapid Format

Official 10+0 Time Control, Touch-Move Offline, and Instant Armageddon Tiebreaks.

1. 10+0 Rapid Control

• Single game per match.
• 10 mins per player (no increment).
• Touch-move strictly enforced offline.

2. Armageddon Tiebreak

• Played immediately if drawn.
• White gets 5 mins, Black gets 4 mins.
• If draw, Black wins match.

3. Offline Final Board

• Hybrid rounds on Courtyard & Chess.com.
• Grand Final strictly offline on physical board.
• 5-min grace period before forfeit.
