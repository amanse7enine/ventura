📜 Official Rules & Guidelines

Participants must abide by the collegiate sports code of conduct.

  • Online Registration & Fee: Registrations are online only. Participation fee is ₹30/- per player (Participation fee of unsold players will be fully refunded).
  • Player Auction System: Auction will feature 3 player tiers: Gold, Silver, and Bronze. Points system will be disclosed prior to the auction. Min/Max player limits per team decided post-registration (teams below minimum quota will be disqualified).
  • 3-Stage Auction Rounds:
    • Stage 1: All registered players go under the hammer.
    • Stage 2: Unsold players from round 1 get one final chance.
    • Stage 3: Teams provide names for shortlisted final bidding (no further rounds thereafter).
  • Match Ball & Equipment: All matches played with a Leather Ball. Match equipment provided by the Sports Committee (optional to bring personal accessories like L-guard, supporter, etc.).
  • Pool Format: 2 Pools with 3 teams in each pool. In the pool stage, each team plays 2 matches against every other team in the same pool.
  • Match Overs & Powerplay:
    • Pool Matches, Qualifier & Eliminator: 10 overs per innings (Powerplay: First 3 overs).
    • Grand Final: 15 overs per innings (Powerplay: First 5 overs).
  • Official Playing Conditions: Standard ICC rules enforced EXCEPT NO LBW and NO DRS. Umpire's decision is absolute and final.
  • Playoff Qualification Rules:
    • The Qualifier: Top team from Pool A vs Top team from Pool B. Winner advances directly to the Final.
    • The Eliminator: Loser of Qualifier vs best-ranked team among remaining teams from both pools.
    • Grand Final: Winner of Qualifier vs Winner of Eliminator.
  • Rule Amendments: Any additional rules or modifications will be conveyed through the official WhatsApp groups.

⚡ Playoff & Qualification System

2 Pools with 3 Teams each. Each team plays 2 matches against pool opponents in the preliminary stage.

1. The Qualifier (10 Overs)

Top Pool A vs Top Pool B.
• Winner: Directly to Grand Final
• Loser: Gets second chance in Eliminator

2. The Eliminator (10 Overs)

Loser of Qualifier vs Best-Ranked remaining team from both pools.
• Winner: Advances to Grand Final
• Loser: Knocked out

3. Grand Final (15 Overs)

Winner of Qualifier vs Winner of Eliminator.
• 5 Overs Powerplay.
• Champion: Crowned Ventura 2k26 Victor!
