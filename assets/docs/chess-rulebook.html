RULES FOR VENTURA 2026 CHESS TOURNAMENT

Annual Sports Meet • IIIT Vadodara • Ventura 2k26

Official Rapid Knockout Draft

1. Registration Rules

  • Registrations will be online.
  • Registration deadline: 18th October 2026.
  • Participation fee: Rs. 20/-
  • Entry is individual. One player per entry.
  • At registration, every player must choose the mode: Offline (physical board at venue) or Online (on Chess.com).
  • Online players must provide their correct Chess.com username at registration and use only their college email id.

2. Tournament Format

  • The tournament is a single-elimination knockout. The winner of a match advances; the loser is out.
  • The bracket will be drawn randomly by the committee after registration closes.
  • The bracket size will be the next power of 2 above the number of players (empty slots filled with byes).
  • Byes will be given only in Round 1 and distributed evenly across the bracket. No player gets a bye twice.
  • Rounds proceed from Round 1, Round 2 to Quarter-Finals, Semi-Finals, and the Final.
  • The Final will be played offline on a physical board in front of the committee.

3. Game's Rules & Regulation

  • Each match is a single game of chess.
  • Time control: 10 minutes per player with no increment (10+0).
  • Colors for each match decided by committee or coin toss. All official FIDE rules apply.
  • Touch-move rule applies in offline matches: a piece touched must be moved; released piece must stay.
  • The arbiter's decision will be final. Misconduct or arguing with arbiter leads to game loss or disqualification.

4. Draw and Tiebreak Rules (Armageddon)

Armageddon Game: A drawn game will be settled by an Armageddon game played immediately. In Armageddon, colors are swapped. White gets 5 minutes and Black gets 4 minutes, with no increment. If the Armageddon game is drawn, Black wins the match.

Draws by agreement are allowed only after move 20. Before that, the draw must be approved by the arbiter.

5. Offline & Online Playing Conditions

  • Offline Matches: Boards, pieces, and digital clocks provided by committee. Players must report to table at announced time (more than 5 minutes late loses by forfeit). Mobile phones must be switched off and kept away. Spectators must stay silent.
  • Online Matches: Played on Chess.com. Opponents paired by username; designated player creates 10+0 unrated challenge. More than 5 minutes late loses by forfeit. Winner submits game link/screenshot immediately. Use of chess engines, databases, or outside assistance strictly prohibited (disqualification for cheating).
  • Matches Between Online and Offline Players: If chosen modes differ, the committee decides the mode and informs both players in advance.
  • No-Show: Opponent wins by walkover if a player fails to appear. Players not playing due to committee error will receive a fee refund.
