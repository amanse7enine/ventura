🏐 VOLLEYBALL TOURNAMENT — OFFICIAL RULEBOOK

VENTURA 2k26 • INDIAN INSTITUTE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY VADODARA

OFFICIAL & FINAL

1. Registration Rules

  • Online Registration: Registrations will be strictly online via the official Ventura portal.
  • Participation Fee: Rs. 30/- per player.
  • Fee Refund Policy: Participation fees paid by unsold players will be fully refunded.

2. Auction Details & Squad Composition

  • Auction Schedule: Auction date and time will be announced by the organising committee.
  • Team Composition: Each team will consist of 7 players.
  • Mandatory Female Player: At least 1 girl is mandatory in every team squad and on the court at all times.
  • Team Size Rule: Minimum and maximum number of players per team will follow the team-size rule announced by the organising committee. A team that does not meet the required team composition, including the mandatory girl player, may be disqualified under the tournament rules.
  • Player Categories: Players will be divided into three categories: Gold, Silver, and Bronze. The points system will be announced before the auction.
  • 3-Stage Auction Process:
    1. First Stage: Auction of all registered players.
    2. Second Stage: Auction of all unsold players from the first stage (unsold players get one more chance; no further auction round held after that).
    3. Third Stage: Each team may submit names of selected players for another auction (limit announced after the second stage).

3. Game Rules and Regulations

  • Matches will follow standard indoor volleyball rules, except for tournament-specific changes announced by the organising committee.
  • Court Presence: Each team will consist of 7 players at a time in the court and minimum 1 girl is mandatory in the court.
  • Teams must follow official match rules.
  • Authority: The referee's decision will be absolute and final.

4. Match Format & Set Scores

Tournament Stage Format Set Point Breakdown
Pool Matches Best of 3 Sets 15 – 15 – 21 Points
Semi-Finals Best of 3 Sets 21 – 21 – 25 Points
Championship Grand Final Best of 5 Sets 21 – 21 – 21 – 21 – 25 Points

* The organising committee may announce match-duration, court, substitution, or other event-specific rules before the tournament.

5. Tournament Structure & Qualification (10 Teams)

  • 2 Pools Allocation: 10 teams divided into 2 Pools (Pool A & Pool B) with 5 teams in each pool.
  • Round-Robin Matches: Each team will play every other team in its own pool once (4 pool matches per team).
  • Pool Standings: Pool A and Pool B standings will be calculated separately based on match wins.
  • Semi-Finals Qualification: The top 2 teams from each pool will qualify for the semi-finals:
    • Semi-Final 1: Pool A 1st Place vs Pool B 2nd Place.
    • Semi-Final 2: Pool B 1st Place vs Pool A 2nd Place.
  • Finals: The winners of both semi-finals will qualify for the Final. (The two semi-final losers may play a 3rd-place match if scheduled).

6. Pool Ranking & Tie-Breaks

  • Pool standings will be determined by match wins.
  • If teams are tied, the organising committee will apply tie-break criteria announced before the tournament, such as set ratio and point ratio.
  • NOTE: The organising committee may introduce or revise rules when necessary. Any updates will be communicated through the official WhatsApp groups.
