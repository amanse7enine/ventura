1. Registration & Team Formation Rules

  • Player registration will be conducted online. Participation fee is ₹20 per player.
  • Each team owner will pay ₹200. Owners are not eligible to play for the teams they own.
  • Owners will select their team captain.
  • Team formation will take place through an auction on Wednesday, 21 October 2026.
  • Each team must have a squad of 13–15 players, including substitutes. Eleven players take the field for an 11-a-side match.

3. Match Format & Qualification

  • 8 Teams, 2 Groups: Group A and Group B (4 teams each). Shortened schedule: each team plays 2 group-stage matches.
  • Points System: Win: 3 points | Draw: 1 point | Loss: 0 points.
  • Tie-Breakers: Goal difference, then goals scored, then head-to-head result.
  • Semi-Finals: Top 2 teams from each group qualify.
    • Semi-Final 1: Group A 1st place (A1) vs Group B 2nd place (B2).
    • Semi-Final 2: Group B 1st place (B1) vs Group A 2nd place (A2).
  • The winners of both semi-finals qualify for the Final. No third-place match will be played.

4. Match Duration & Tie Resolution

  • Group-Stage & Semi-Finals: Two halves of 20 minutes each. Half-time break: 5 minutes.
  • Grand Final: Two halves of 25 minutes each. Half-time break: 5 minutes.
  • Tied Knockout Matches: Decided by a standard penalty shootout (5 kicks per team, followed by sudden death).

5. Game Rules, Substitutions & Discipline

  • Matches follow standard football rules. The standard offside rule applies.
  • Rolling Substitutions: Permitted with a maximum of three players substituted per team per match.
  • Discipline: 2 yellow cards in the same match result in a red card (player leaves, team plays with one fewer).
  • Strict Kickoff: Teams must be ready at scheduled time. No grace period provided. Failure to appear results in a 3–0 walkover.
  • Winner and runner-up trophies will be awarded.
