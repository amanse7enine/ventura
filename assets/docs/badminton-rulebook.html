🏸 BADMINTON TOURNAMENT — OFFICIAL RULEBOOK

VENTURA 2026-27 • INDIAN INSTITUTE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY VADODARA

OFFICIAL & FINAL

1. Tournament Overview & Venue

The Ventura Badminton Tournament is organized as an official collegiate tournament at the high-standard indoor wooden courts of Aloka Sports Academy.

  • Venue: Aloka Sports Academy (Indoor Wooden Courts)
  • Tournament Dates: 18th & 20th October
  • Transportation: College buses provided for all participants between campus and Aloka Sports Academy.
  • Official Shuttles: High-grade Yonex Mavis 350 nylon shuttles will be used for all official ties.

2. Categories & Registration

  • Categories:
    1. Men's Singles
    2. Women's Singles
    3. Men's Doubles
    4. Mixed Doubles
  • Registration Deadline: 15th October (Strict Deadline). No entries entertained post-deadline.
  • Doubles Pairing: Both partners must be finalized prior to submitting registration.

3. Date & Batch Match Schedule

Date Slot / Batch Events Scheduled Participation Guidelines
18th October After 02:00 PM Men's Singles & Men's Doubles (Rounds) Senior Batches (2nd, 3rd, 4th Year, Postgrads)
18th October After 06:00 PM Men's Singles & Men's Doubles (Rounds) 1st Year Freshers Only
⚠️ Night Travel Safety Policy: On 18th October, only boys will participate as girls are not permitted to travel to Aloka Sports Academy at night.
20th October Full Day Championship Quarter-Finals, Semi-Finals, Grand Finals, Girls' Singles & Mixed Doubles All Qualified Players across all batches

4. Match Playing Rules & Standards

  • BWF Standards: All matches conducted strictly under Badminton World Federation (BWF) 21-point rally scoring system.
  • Footwear Regulation: Non-marking badminton shoes are strictly mandatory inside the Aloka wooden hall. Players with running shoes or black soles will not be allowed on court.
  • Reporting & Walkover: Players must report 15 minutes before scheduled match slot. Walkover awarded if player fails to appear within 10 minutes of court call.
  • Referee Decision: The decision of the chief umpire and sports committee is absolute and binding.
