RULES FOR TUG OF WAR & ATHLETICS

Annual Sports Meet • IIIT Vadodara • Ventura 2k26

Official Tournament Bylaws

1. Registration Rules & Fees

  • Registrations will be online.
  • Participation Fees:
    • ₹20/- per individual event
    • ₹100/- for Tug of War (5 players)
    • ₹80/- for 4x100m Relay
  • For solo registrations, random teams will be allotted as per the registrations.

2. Game Rules and Regulations

  • Tug of War: Held in the institute premises during college days. League matches between 2 different pools. Each team will consist of 5 players. Every player should be present on the day of their match.
  • Athletics: Held on CRPF ground. It will be conducted in slots with the best timing becoming the winner.
  • Mandatory Outfit: Sports outfit is mandatory to participate.
  • Authority: The referee's decision will be final.

3. Match Format & Tournament Structure

Tug of War League: There will be 2 pools for both girls and boys. Each team will play every other team in its own pool once. Pool A and Pool B standings calculated separately.
  • Match Format (Tug of War):
    • Pool matches: Best of 3 pulls
    • Semi-finals: Best of 3 pulls
    • Final: Best of 3 pulls
  • Match Format (Athletics): Races will be done in slots. Final will be decided on the basis of top best timings.

4. Qualification & Tie-Breaks

  • Playoffs: The top 2 teams from each pool qualify for the semi-finals:
    • Semi-Final 1: Pool A 1st place vs Pool B 2nd place
    • Semi-Final 2: Pool B 1st place vs Pool A 2nd place
  • The winners of both semi-finals will qualify for the Final. (The two semi-final losers may play a third-place match if scheduled).
  • Pool Ranking & Tie-Breaks: Pool standings determined by match wins. If teams are tied, tie-break criteria announced before tournament will apply.

NOTE: The organising committee may introduce or revise rules when necessary. Any updates will be communicated through the official WhatsApp groups.
