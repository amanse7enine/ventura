🏏 CRICKET TOURNAMENT — OFFICIAL RULEBOOK

VENTURA 2k26 • INDIAN INSTITUTE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY VADODARA

OFFICIAL & FINAL

1. General Tournament Overview

The Ventura 2k26 Cricket Championship is an inter-batch tournament played with a Leather Ball under strict playing guidelines, featuring player auctions, round-robin pool stages, and an IPL-style playoff qualification format.

  • Venue: CRPF Ground
  • Tournament Dates: 24th & 31st October (08:00 AM – 06:00 PM)
  • Match Ball: High-grade Leather Ball (provided by Sports Committee)
  • Kit & Equipment: Full tournament equipment provided. Players may bring personal protective gear (L-guard, supporter, personal bat).

2. Registration & Participation Fee

  • Online Registration: All entries must be registered via the official Ventura online portal.
  • Participation Fee: ₹30/- per player.
  • Unsold Player Protection: The participation fee of any player who remains unsold across all auction rounds will be 100% refunded.

3. Player Auction System & Details

  • Player Tiers: Registered players will be categorized into three tiers: Gold, Silver, and Bronze based on verified skill profile.
  • Points System: Team bidding purse and points allocations will be announced prior to the auction.
  • Squad Limits: Minimum and maximum squad caps per team will be finalized post-registration. Any team failing to draft the minimum player count as per the rule will be disqualified.
  • 3-Stage Auction Process:
    1. Stage 1 (Primary Round): All registered players go under the hammer.
    2. Stage 2 (Unsold Round): All unsold players from Round 1 get one final opportunity.
    3. Stage 3 (Shortlisted Nominations): Each team submits specific player names (limit defined after Round 2) for re-auction. No further auction rounds will be held after Stage 3.

4. Match Formats, Overs & Powerplay

Stage / Match Overs Per Innings Mandatory Powerplay Bowler Max Quota
Pool Stage Matches 10 Overs First 3 Overs Max 2 overs per bowler
The Qualifier 10 Overs First 3 Overs Max 2 overs per bowler
The Eliminator 10 Overs First 3 Overs Max 2 overs per bowler
Championship Grand Final 15 Overs First 5 Overs Max 3 overs per bowler

5. Tournament Structure & Pool Matches

  • Pool Allocation: 6 teams divided into 2 Pools (Pool A & Pool B) with 3 teams in each pool.
  • Pool Round-Robin: In the pool stage, each team will play 2 matches against every other team in the same pool.

6. Playoff Qualification Rules

  1. The Qualifier: The top-ranked team from Pool A plays against the top-ranked team from Pool B.
  2. Direct Final Entry: The winner of the Qualifier advances directly to the Championship Grand Final.
  3. Second Chance: The loser of the Qualifier will play in The Eliminator.
  4. The Eliminator: Played between the loser of the Qualifier and the best-ranked team among the remaining teams from both pools.
  5. Grand Final: The winner of the Eliminator qualifies for the Final against the Winner of the Qualifier.

7. Playing Rules & Exceptions

  • All standard ICC playing conditions apply with college ground local boundary rules.
  • EXCEPTIONS: Strictly NO LBW and NO DRS.
  • Umpire Authority: The on-field umpire's decision is absolute and final. Any dissent will attract immediate penalty points.
  • Amendments: Any situational updates or new guidelines will be conveyed directly through official WhatsApp groups.
