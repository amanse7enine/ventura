VENTURA 2K26 — TABLE TENNIS RULEBOOK

Annual Sports Meet • IIIT Vadodara (TENEX Leagues)

Official Tournament Draft

1. Tournament Categories

  • Men's Singles: Individual event for male participants.
  • Men's Doubles: Event for pairs of male participants.
  • Women's Singles: Individual event for female participants.
  • Women's Doubles: Event for pairs of female participants.
  • Mixed Doubles: Each pair must consist of one male and one female participant.

2. Match Rules & Format

  • Every match will be played as best of 3 games, with each game played to 11 points. The player or pair that wins 2 games wins the match.
  • At 10–10, the game will continue until one player or pair leads by 2 points.
  • Before the match, the umpire/referee will conduct a toss to decide the initial serve/receive and playing side.
  • A 5-minute warm-up will be allowed before the match begins.
  • Players must report to the playing area at least 10 minutes before their scheduled match.
  • Grace Period: A 10-minute grace period is allowed. If still not arrived, the opponent is declared winner by walkover.
  • Each player may request up to two time-outs during a match.

3. Service & Play

16 cm Service Rule: The server must release the ball at least 16 cm vertically from the open palm when serving, as stated in the supplied TENEX league rulebook. Illegal service will receive umpire warnings and point penalties.
  • A double touch by the same player during a stroke will be treated as a fault (point awarded to opponent).
  • The umpire/referee's decision on match play will be final.

4. Doubles-Specific Rules

  • In doubles, the serve must travel diagonally from server's right half-court to receiver's right half-court.
  • Partners must take turns striking the ball during a rally.
  • The serving and receiving order must be followed throughout the game.

5. Player Conduct & Organization

  • Participants must treat opponents, officials, and volunteers respectfully. Unsporting behaviour leads to penalties or disqualification.
  • Players should bring their own racket and wear suitable sportswear and footwear.
  • Fixtures and match timings will be shared through the official TENEX WhatsApp group.
