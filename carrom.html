📜 Official Rules & Guidelines

Participants must abide by the collegiate sports code of conduct.

  • Tournament Categories: Singles (1 player vs 1 player) and Doubles (2 players vs 2 players). Open to eligible B.Tech, MCA, and M.Tech students across all batches.
  • Match Format & Draw: Knockout tournament format. Matches are recommended Best of 3 boards (first to win 2 boards wins the match). Grand Final may be Best of 5 boards. Participants not reporting within the announced grace period will forfeit.
  • Equipment & Seating: Organizers provide tournament board, carrom men, and approved striker. In singles, opponents sit opposite each other; in doubles, teammates sit opposite each other. Players must not lift, tilt, shake, or move the board during play.
  • Basic Playing Rules: Toss / referee method decides who starts. Striker must be placed within permitted baseline area and flicked with a finger (pushing, throwing, or illegal propelling is strictly prohibited). Turn continues after legally pocketing own coin; otherwise passes to opponent. Touching or repositioning coins by hand is forbidden. Referee restores coins leaving the board.
  • The Queen (Red Coin): The red carrom man is the Queen. The Queen must be covered by legally pocketing one of player's/team's own carrom men on the same turn or the next eligible turn. If uncovered, the Queen is returned to the center by the referee.
  • Fouls & Penalties: Pocketing the striker is a foul. Making an illegal strike, playing outside the baseline, striking out of turn, or disturbing the board ends the turn. Where required, a previously pocketed carrom man is returned to the center as a penalty coin.
  • Scoring System: A board is won when a player/team legally pockets all their own carrom men, subject to the Queen rule. Winner receives 1 point for each opposing carrom man left on board. Legally pocketing and covering the Queen awards 3 additional points. Any scoring objections must be raised before the next board begins.
  • Doubles Regulations: Both teammates must be registered and eligible. Teammates sit opposite and take turns in referee-directed order. Teammates may communicate between turns, but must never distract an opponent during a strike. Substitutions mid-match are not permitted.
  • Conduct & Sportsmanship: Respect opponents, teammates, referees, and organizers. Abusive language, cheating, or intentional distraction leads to warning or disqualification. Spectators must not interfere with matches.
  • Disputes & Final Authority: Objections must be raised immediately before the next turn starts. Referee on-board decisions are final for the event. Sports Committee – IIITV (GN) holds final operational and administrative authority.

🎯 Tournament Format & Board Rules

Official Singles & Doubles Knockout Tournament. Conducted in Institute Premises.

1. Knockout Structure

• Singles (1v1) & Doubles (2v2).
• Opening & Quarters: Best of 3 Boards (first to 2).
• Grand Final: Best of 5 Boards.

2. Queen & Covering Rule

• Queen (Red): +3 Points.
• Must be covered on same or next eligible turn.
• If uncovered, returned to center by referee.

3. Scoring & Penalties

• 1 point per opposing coin left on board.
• Pocketing striker = Foul + 1 penalty coin.
• Referee on-board decision final.
