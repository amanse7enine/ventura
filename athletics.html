📜 Official Rules & Guidelines

Participants must abide by the collegiate sports code of conduct.

  • Registration & Entry Fees: Registrations will be conducted online. Participation fee is ₹20/- per individual event, ₹80/- for 4x100m relay, and ₹100/- for tug of war. For solo registrations, random teams will be allotted as per the registrations.
  • Tug of War Venue & Schedule: Tug of war will be held in the institute premises during college days. Matches will be conducted between 2 different pools (for boys and girls separately).
  • Tug of War Squad Composition: Each team will consist of 5 players. Every registered player should be present on the day of their match.
  • Athletics Venue & Timing System: Athletics events will be held on the CRPF ground. It will be conducted in slots with the best timing becoming the winner. Finals will be decided on the basis of top best timings.
  • Mandatory Dress Code: Sports outfit is strictly mandatory to participate in all events. Spikes are permitted for track sprints on designated track lanes.
  • Tournament Structure (Tug of War): There will be 2 pools for both girls and boys. Each team will play every other team in its own pool once. Pool A and Pool B standings will be calculated separately.
  • Tug of War Match Format:
    • Pool Matches: Best of 3 pulls.
    • Semi-Finals: Best of 3 pulls.
    • Final: Best of 3 pulls.
  • Playoffs & Qualification (Tug of War): The top 2 teams from each pool qualify for the semi-finals.
    • Semi-Final 1: Pool A 1st place vs Pool B 2nd place.
    • Semi-Final 2: Pool B 1st place vs Pool A 2nd place.
    • Winners of both semi-finals advance to the Final. (The two semi-final losers may play a 3rd-place match if scheduled).
  • Pool Standings & Tie-Breaks: Pool standings determined by match wins. If teams are tied, tie-break criteria announced before the tournament will apply.
  • Authority & Communication: The referee's decision will be final. The organising committee may introduce or revise rules when necessary; all updates will be communicated through official WhatsApp groups.

⚡ Tug of War & Athletics Competition Structure

Official 2-Pool Tug of War League & CRPF Ground Track Timings.

1. Tug of War (5v5 - Best of 3)

• 2 Pools (Separate for Boys & Girls).
• 5 Players per team.
• Pool matches & Finals: Best of 3 pulls.

2. Athletics (Best Timings)

• Venue: CRPF Ground.
• Conducted in timing slots.
• Winner decided by top recorded timings.

3. Entry Fees & Playoff Path

• ₹20/individual, ₹80/relay, ₹100/tug of war.
• Tug of War: Top 2 from each pool advance to SF (A1 vs B2, B1 vs A2).
• Sports outfit mandatory.
