📜 Official Rules & Guidelines

Participants must abide by the collegiate sports code of conduct.

  • Organizers & Registration: Organized by the Gym and Athletics Club, IIIT Vadodara. Registrations are officially open for both Athletics and Tug of War.
  • 🏃‍♂️ 1. Athletics (Track & Field):
    • Track Events: 100m, 200m, 4×100m Relay.
    • Special Event: Hybrid Fitness Challenge.
    • Step up and prove you're the fastest and strongest on campus.
    • 👉 Lock in your spot: Track & Field Form
  • 💥 2. Tug of War (The Ultimate Power Clash):
    • Team Registrations: Already got your squad? Sign up together and hold the line.
    • Solo Registrations (No Team? No Problem!): Want to pull but missing a crew? Sign up solo—we'll place you into a powerhouse team!
    • 👉 Lock in your spot: Solo Form | Team Form
  • 📌 The Rundown:
    • Venue: CRPF Ground (Athletics) & Institute Premises (Tug Of War).
    • Dates: 15 Oct – 1 Nov (Tug of War) & 31 Oct – 01 Nov (Athletics).
  • ⏳ Registration Deadlines: 18 Oct for Tug of War & 22 Oct for Athletics.
  • 🏆 Awards: Medals, prizes, and ultimate campus bragging rights await.
  • Mandatory Dress Code: Sports outfit strictly mandatory to participate in all events. Spikes permitted on designated track lanes.
  • Match Conduct: Referee decisions are final. Tug of war conducted in Best of 3 pulls format.

⚡ Gym & Athletics Club • Competition Structure

Organized by the Gym and Athletics Club, IIIT Vadodara • Official Track & Field and Tug of War Championships.

1. Athletics (Track & Field)

• Track: 100m, 200m, 4×100m Relay.
• Special Event: Hybrid Fitness Challenge.
• Venue: CRPF Ground (31 Oct & 01 Nov).
• Deadline: 22 Oct.

2. Tug of War (Power Clash)

• Team Registrations: Squad sign-up.
• Solo Registrations: Powerhouse team allotment.
• Venue: Institute Premises (15 Oct – 01 Nov).
• Deadline: 18 Oct.

3. Awards & Rules

• Medals, prizes & campus bragging rights.
• Best of 3 pulls in Tug of War matches.
• Sports outfit mandatory; spikes permitted on track.
