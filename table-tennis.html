📜 Official Rules & Guidelines

Participants must abide by the collegiate sports code of conduct.

  • Tournament Categories: Men's Singles, Men's Doubles, Women's Singles, Women's Doubles, and Mixed Doubles (each pair must consist of one male and one female participant).
  • Match Format: Every match will be played as best of 3 games, with each game played to 11 points. The player or pair that wins 2 games wins the match. At 10–10 (deuce), the game continues until one player or pair leads by 2 points.
  • Pre-Match Protocol: Toss conducted by umpire/referee decides initial serve/receive and playing side. A 5-minute warm-up is allowed before play begins. Players must report at least 10 minutes prior.
  • Grace Period & Walkovers: A 10-minute grace period is allowed for late arrivals. If a player/pair fails to arrive within 10 minutes, the opponent is awarded a walkover victory.
  • Time-Outs: Each player/pair may request up to two time-outs during a match, subject to the umpire's instructions.
  • Service & Ball Release Rule: The server must release the ball at least 16 cm vertically from the open palm when serving, as stated in the official TENEX league rulebook. Illegal service will receive warnings and point penalties.
  • Faults & Strokes: A double touch by the same player during a stroke is treated as a fault, and a point is awarded to the opponent.
  • Doubles Regulations: Serve must travel diagonally from server's right half-court to receiver's right half-court. Partners must take turns striking the ball during a rally. Strict serving and receiving order must be followed.
  • Tournament Structure & Qualification: Categories organized into pools, knockout rounds, or a combination. Top performers from pools advance to quarter-finals and playoffs.
  • Equipment & Conduct: Players should bring their own racket and wear suitable sportswear and non-marking footwear. All umpire and referee decisions on match play are final.

🏓 TENEX Table Tennis Championship Rules

Official Best of 3 Games to 11 Points • 5 Categories • 16 cm Vertical Service Rule.

1. Match Structure

• Best of 3 games to 11 points (first to 2).
• Deuce at 10-10 requires 2-point lead.
• Up to 2 time-outs per match.

2. 16cm Service & Play

• Ball released min 16cm vertically from open palm.
• Double touch = fault (point to opponent).
• 10-min grace period before walkover.

3. Doubles & Mixed

• Diagonal serve (right-to-right).
• Alternating hits by teammates during rally.
• Non-marking shoes & own racket.
